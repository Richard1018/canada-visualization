Canada: Indigenous Population and Community Health

Interactive visualization of the provided 2021 population and survey data

1. Indigenous Population Across Canada

Switch between the four map layers. The first shows population counts; the other three show percentages of each region's total population. Hover over a region for exact counts and percentages.

2. Community Belonging and Mental-Health Needs Met

Use the animation controls to compare sexes. Color and symbol indicate group. Only observations with both percentages available are plotted.

3. Indigenous Group Composition in the Three Largest Regions

Each donut shows the distribution of First Nations, Inuit, and Métis within the Indigenous population of the indicated province.

Source: Instructor-provided 2021 Canada population and health-inequality data. Missing percentages were not treated as zero.
